About Me

I am a Data Science undergraduate at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (Entrepreneur College, Taicang). My work sits at the intersection of data-driven computing and thoughtful software engineering—combining distributed analytics frameworks with responsive, user-respecting local applications.
